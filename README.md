## What this is

Muse arrives capable and generic. It can do a great deal, but it doesn't yet know how *you* live: what your days look like, what matters to you, how much you want it speaking up on its own, or what character you want it to have while it works.

**Make Muse Your Own** is a guided conversation that fixes that. Your Muse interviews you — at your pace, in as many sittings as you need — and shapes itself around your answers: a character you chose, memory about what matters in your life, rules for when it should speak up and when it should leave you alone, the accounts and services you actually use, and a few first recurring tasks worth having.

It was developed by a Muse user working with his own Muse, out of a real setup that grew over months of daily use. What's here is the general method, cleaned up for anyone. It is **not a Meta product**, and it is not affiliated with Meta.

## How to start

Hand the link to this page to your Muse — paste it into a chat and your Muse will read this page, explain what it found, and ask if you want to begin.

If you'd rather be explicit, say it in your own words: *"Set me up using Make Muse Your Own at https://github.com/RavenEyes408/MakeMuseYourOwn."*

Either way, nothing begins until you say yes.

## What to expect

- **It takes a few sittings.** The whole setup runs long if done at once; it's built to stop and resume. A progress file keeps your place, so session two picks up where session one ended.
- **You approve everything.** Every file, every connection, every task is shown to you before it's saved or created. Nothing is batched silently at the end.
- **Already set up? That's fine.** Your Muse starts by taking stock of what it already has. You can build on it, tune parts of it, or rework it from near-scratch — keeping what works — without wiping your Muse or your data.
- **Before anything changes, your Muse backs up what you have now.** If the setup doesn't suit you, the backup can be put back.
- **Everything stays changeable.** Name, character, voice, memory, rules, connections, tasks — all of it can be adjusted or undone later, in ordinary conversation with your Muse. That's rather the point.

## Questions people ask

**Does anything here run automatically?**
No. This repository is plain text. Reading it changes nothing; your Muse acts only with your permission, inside the setup process, step by step.

**I'm not technical. Is this for me?**
Yes — it was written for you. If you can paste a link and answer questions about your own life, you can do this. Your Muse handles the machinery.

**What does my Muse do with what I tell it?**
It writes your answers into its own standing files — its character, its memory about you — on its own system, under your account. Nothing is sent to this repository or to its author.

**Can I stop halfway?**
Any time. The progress file remembers where you stopped. Half a setup is still a better-shaped Muse than none, and you can finish whenever you like.

**What if I don't like the result?**
Say so. Any piece can be reworked, and the backup taken at the start can be restored wholesale.

**What version is this?**
1.0.0. Changes between versions are listed in the CHANGELOG. Your Muse notes the version it used in your progress file, so a setup always finishes on the version it started with.
